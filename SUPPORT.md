# AskIt Support

Support is handled through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/batuhanberk06/askit/issues).

Before opening an issue:

1. Confirm that Chrome is version 127 or newer.
2. Reload AskIt from `chrome://extensions`.
3. Confirm that the selected AI website opens normally and that any required sign-in is complete.
4. Try the same action once more on a regular `https://` page.

When reporting a problem, include:

- AskIt version from `chrome://extensions`;
- Chrome version and operating system;
- selected AI provider;
- whether the input was selected text, a page URL, or a screenshot;
- the task selected in AskIt;
- the exact error message, if one appeared; and
- clear reproduction steps.

Do not post selected private text, screenshots, credentials, session details, or personal information. Replace sensitive examples with harmless sample content.

## Known limitations

- AskIt cannot capture or share restricted browser pages such as `chrome://` pages.
- AI providers can change their composer interfaces. Text or image insertion may temporarily stop working until AskIt is updated.
- Screenshot attachment is not guaranteed. AskIt offers a copy/drag fallback when it can detect that automatic attachment failed.
- The AI provider may require an account, sign-in, or acceptance of its own terms.

For privacy details, read the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
